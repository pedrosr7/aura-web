How it works

Five steps, from invite to how it landed.

01 · Invite

One link. Your team is in.

Share it wherever you already talk: WhatsApp, Instagram, the box group. Whoever opens it joins your team, and you watch them arrive.

  • One link for the whole team
  • A heads-up when someone joins
  • See who got in, and when

02 · Group

RX, Scaled, Masters… or one to one.

Make the groups you already have in your head and tick who is in each. Programming a group weighs the same as programming one athlete.

  • Every group has its own week
  • An athlete can be in several groups
  • 1:1 coaching when you need it

03 · Program

Write it the way you always do. Aura builds it.

Paste the text, dictate it or snap the whiteboard. Aura reads it and turns it into blocks, clocks and loads. You decide when they see it.

  • Text, voice or photo
  • Blocks and clocks: AMRAP, EMOM, For Time…
  • On save, or the evening before at the hour you pick

04 · It lands

An instant heads-up. Their kilos, already worked out.

As soon as the session is in view, the athlete gets the alert and sees it in their Pulse. Loads come from their own PR: 75 % of 80 kg is 60 kg, no calculator.

  • Clock and logger that fit each block
  • Their history and PRs, always with them
  • Planning is included: your athletes do not pay for PRO

The alert is a recreation of the one Aura sends.

05 · How it landed

Who did it, and what to look at first.

By day and by week: how many trained, how hard it felt and which block got away from them. “Watch” tells you where to start.

  • RPE and feeling by block
  • Group loads, from the lightest to the heaviest
  • The week against the one before

FAQ

What coaches usually ask.

Do my athletes have to pay?

No. They join with your link and planning is included, no PRO needed.

What does it cost me?

Up to 3 athletes is free. Bigger teams have plans by number of athletes. If you run a large group and want to try it properly, write to me.

What do I need to start?

The app on iOS or Android. When you create the account you pick “I coach others” and your team link is ready.

Can I keep writing the week the way I do now?

Yes. Paste the text, dictate it or take a photo and Aura builds it. You can adjust any block in the editor.

Shall we try it with your team?

Start free with up to 3 athletes. If you run a bigger group, write to me and we will set it up together.
