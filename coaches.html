How it works

Five steps, from invite to how it landed.

Real app screens, with sample data. The alert in step 4 is a recreation of the one Aura sends.

  1. Share it wherever you already talk: WhatsApp, Instagram, the box group. Whoever opens it joins your team, and you watch them arrive.

  2. Make the groups you already have in your head and tick who is in each. Programming a group weighs the same as programming one athlete.

  3. Paste the text, dictate it or snap the whiteboard. Aura reads it and turns it into blocks, clocks and loads. You decide when they see it.

  4. As soon as the session is in view, the athlete gets the alert and sees it in their Pulse. Loads come from their own PR: 75 % of 80 kg is 60 kg, no calculator.

  5. By day and by week: how many trained, how hard it felt and which block got away from them. “Watch” tells you where to start.

FAQ

What coaches usually ask.

Do my athletes have to pay?

No. They join with your link and planning is included, no PRO needed.

What does it cost me?

Up to 3 athletes, nothing. After that, €19.99 a month up to 20 athletes, €39.99 up to 60 and €79.99 up to 200. If you have more, write to me and we'll talk.

What do I need to start?

The app on iOS or Android. When you create the account you pick “I coach others” and your team link is ready.

Can I keep writing the week the way I do now?

Yes. Paste the text, dictate it or take a photo and Aura builds it. You can adjust any block in the editor.

Shall we try it with your team?

Start free with up to 3 athletes: download Aura and, when you create the account, pick “I coach others”.
